## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
The owner moderates scraped Indian shopping offers, checks product/price/coupon/source evidence, edits a Telegram post, and publishes to configured destinations. The app also manages sources, automatic rules and deal videos.

## Capabilities and constraints
Preserve Review card grid and Inspector, edit/draft/approve/skip, bulk actions, filters, source/affiliate/image tools, sharing, Published history, Channels, Heatmap, rules/simulator, Videos/Creator Kit, Settings and login. Existing backend endpoints and authorization remain; no deployment or production mutations as visual verification. Keep unrelated local changes. Function map: docs/design/FUNCTION-MAP.md.

## Confirmed design intent
Owner explicitly requested a custom whole-site design; rejected basic light list mockups, generic recoloring, cyberpunk badge/glow clutter and copying IndiaDealHunts. Supplied populated screenshot establishes dark product-image card grid, compact sidebar and rapid triage as reference. All screens need coherent care, not just Review.
