INDIADEALHUNTS / ALL-PAGE DESIGN TEARDOWN / 28 OCTOBER 2026

A shared design problem, with different fixes for each task.

Coverage: all 10 main tab views including the homepage, 12 planning tools, plus search, product details, comparison, lookup, saved targets, alerts, filters and legal surfaces. This is a visual and information-hierarchy audit, not a full functional certification.

Desktop captures: 1280 × 720. Mobile captures: 393 × 852. Known routes were mapped from App.tsx; real UI controls were used for search, saving and product details. Comparison and alert fixtures are explicitly marked. No messages, alerts, deals or purchases were submitted.

Product details and price history

123

Directory offer, Verified Flipkart, savings vs MRP and DealFlow Verified Price History appear in different parts of the same detail view. Explain exactly what each label verifies.

2. Priority

The photo, full merchant title, discount and large history panel compete. The desktop footer offers merchant, Scan Stores, Copy Link and expiry actions.
