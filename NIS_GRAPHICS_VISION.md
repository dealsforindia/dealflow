# NIS Graphics — Vision

6 Nov 2026. Studio brand, not a personal portfolio. Nilotpal Saha is the desk;
NIS Graphics is the name clients hire.

---

## 1. The positioning, in one paragraph

**NIS Graphics is a one-desk commercial design studio in Kolkata that runs a
client's design calendar for the year.** Festival campaigns, fare drops,
admission seasons, clinic offers, weekly social. Print-first, motion-ready,
deadline-true. Not an agency, not a freelancer — a studio you keep on retainer
because the dates don't move.

## 2. The insight

Look at what is actually in the folder. Every single piece is attached to a date:

| Date-driven | Piece |
| --- | --- |
| Durga Puja | Image Clinic campaign, 4 variants |
| Ram Navami | Gill Travels Australia |
| Holi | festival greeting |
| Nurses Day | Image Clinic |
| Admission season | EtuTor, IBDP |
| Fare window | Manila $820 / $1220, India $645 / $965 |
| Music Fest, 20 January | A.G.C. Bose College |

He has never once been asked for "a beautiful composition". He is asked for
**the thing that has to be out by Thursday.** That is the business, and no
competitor's portfolio says it.

## 3. The idea

> **The calendar is the brief.**

A generalist studio organises its site by client. NIS Graphics organises by
**date**, because that is how the work is actually commissioned.

This is not a decoration. It is the only structure that makes 50 square posters
read as a body of work instead of 50 repetitions. A year is a story. A grid of
squares is a stock library.

## 4. The commercial teeth

Posters are one-off. **Calendars are retainers.**

Selling "the year" converts a ₹800-per-post client into a monthly account: twelve
festival posts, four fare campaigns, two reels, one identity refresh. Same work,
better contract. The site's job is to make that shift feel obvious — so the
portfolio shows a *year of output for one client*, not twelve unrelated logos.

## 5. The mark

**N!S.** The exclamation is already in the name and already on every watermark.

An exclamation mark is what a poster does: it announces. Own it as the studio's
only graphic device — section heads, the call to action, the date stamp. One
mark, used everywhere, never redrawn.

Wordmark: `N!S` set tight, `GRAPHICS` beneath in letterspaced mono.

## 6. Voice

Short sentences. Dates, quantities, formats. No adjectives about ourselves.

| Write this | Not this |
| --- | --- |
| "Durga Puja campaign, four posts, three days." | "A soulful festive visual journey." |
| "Send the brief. We'll hit the date." | "Let's craft something extraordinary together." |
| "Print files, CMYK, bleed checked." | "Pixel-perfect artistry." |

**Brand line:** *Every date has a design.*
**CTA line:** *Send the brief. We'll hit the date.*
**Retainer line:** *We run the calendar.*

## 7. Visual language — "Press Proof"

The surface is a print studio's proof sheet. The work supplies all the colour.

```
paper      #F4EFE4   cream stock
ink        #14110D   near-black
gold       #C08A2E   from the fare posters
vermilion  #B03A2E   accent, under 2% of surface
rule       #D8CFBC   hairlines
```

| Role | Face | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Bodoni Moda | Announcements, section heads |
| Text | Archivo | Body, labels, nav |
| Mono | IBM Plex Mono | Dates, job numbers, formats |

**The unifying device — the slug.** Every image carries a small mono slug in the
corner, exactly like a proof sheet or a clapperboard:

```
GILL TRAVELS · 12.03.25 · 1080×1080
IMAGE CLINIC · 09.10.25 · 1080×1350
```

This is the fix for the hardest problem on the site: 50 heterogeneous images
that share no visual system. The slug gives them one. It also proves the work is
real and dated.

**Aspect truth.** His work is 1:1 squares and wide banners. The wall shows each
piece at its true ratio on a registration grid — no uniform cards, no cropping.

## 8. The site, in seven screens

| # | Screen | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Masthead** | `N!S GRAPHICS`, Kolkata. "Every date has a design." The year as a thin strip, today marked. |
| 2 | **The year** | Twelve month dividers. Work sits under its month, true ratio, slugged. This is the portfolio. |
| 3 | **Every client’s best work** | Six accounts, one piece each: Gill Travels, Image Clinic, EtuTor, A.G.C. Bose College, DesignLogo, NIS Graphics. The retainer argument, made by showing one finished piece per account instead of a wall of logos. |
| 4 | **What we make** | Five slots: festival campaigns · print & press files · social systems · video & reels · identity. |
| 5 | **Motion** | Six reels, one player, clip list. |
| 6 | **The desk** | Nilotpal. Five roles, two qualifications, nine tools. Short. |
| 7 | **Start a calendar** | The contact bar from his own posters, promoted to site chrome. Phone, WhatsApp, email. |

Total target ~4,000px. Desktop and 390px both first-class.

## 9. What this vision forbids

- No percentage bars, mastery matrices, scope calculators, star ratings.
- No invented clients. Real: Image Clinic, Pixel Solutionz, Hykez, Arena
  Animation, Yogines Studioo, Gill Travels, EtuTor, DesignLogo, NIS Graphics.
- No "Art Director". The resume says Senior Graphic Designer, five times.
- No 3D showreel until real renders exist. The diploma is a credential, not a section.
- No stock-photo hero. The work is the hero.

## 10. The one decision this creates

The calendar is the spine. But a visitor still needs to browse by client.

**Recommended:** calendar is the default view; a client filter sits in the
masthead as a secondary control. Two ways in, one page.

Alternative: drop the calendar, ship a straight gallery. Faster, safer, and it
gives up the only idea here that a competitor cannot copy in an afternoon.
